The noun phrase naming what one subscription covers: "changes to RFC 9110",
"every new RFC". No lead and no full stop, so a caller can put it in a sentence or
in a list.

Split out of _subscription_line.txt when digests became per subscriber rather than
per subscription: a reader with three matching subscriptions gets one mail listing
three of these, while a confirmation is about one subscription and still wants the
sentence.

Context: subscription, and watched_doc for the rfc kind.

This file deliberately ends without a trailing newline: callers put a full stop
straight after the include, and a newline there wordwraps into " .".
