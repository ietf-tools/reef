The body of one subscription notification. Plain text only, and the subject is
composed in Python rather than here, both following the templates/rpc/mail
convention in Purple.

Context: subscription, events (each with doc_display, change, url), watched_doc
(the rfc kind's document, in prose form), subscriptions_url (may be empty).
The wording per kind lives here rather than in the task because it is prose.

Anything holding text this project does not control is wrapped: a set title is
up to 200 characters of the owner's choosing, and a change line comes from the
datatracker feed, so neither can be trusted to fit a plain-text line.
