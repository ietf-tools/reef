The body of one notification to one subscriber. Plain text only, and the subject is
composed in Python rather than here, both following the templates/rpc/mail
convention in Purple.

Context: reasons (each with subscription and watched_doc), events (each with
doc_display, change, url), subscriptions_url (may be empty).

One mail per subscriber rather than per subscription, so reasons is a list: somebody
who follows RFC 9110 directly and also has it in a set hears about a change to it
once, and is told both reasons why. One reason reads as a sentence; several read as
a list, because "you asked to be notified about changes to RFC 9110 and about
anything in your document set" runs out of breath at three.
